## Platform

web

## Users

Broad audience, technical creators, engineering leaders, and the general public seeking an interactive, cinematic demonstration of modern artificial intelligence and 3D web craftsmanship.

## Product Purpose

ORBIT is a digital technological universe and personal portfolio representing Muzammil Yasir (AI Engineer / Researcher / Systems Thinker). It communicates three interconnected dimensions:
1. **The Engineer**: intelligent systems, AI architecture, multi-agent networks, RAG pipelines, computer vision, and real-world edge deployments.
2. **The Researcher**: empirical inquiry, edge intelligence sensing, citation verification, and emerging technologies.
3. **The Individual**: curiosity, systems philosophy, craftsmanship, and aesthetic intentionality.

Success means presenting a distinctive, memorable, and art-directed digital environment that feels like an interactive scientific installation built around one mind.

## Positioning

An immersive spatial intelligence experience uniting **Cinematic 3D Futurism × Premium Minimalism × Interactive Storytelling**. Unlike conventional résumé websites or generic SaaS developer templates, ORBIT is an interactive digital archive with real-time 3D WebGL stages, a cybernetic digital twin, and a relational neural ecosystem.

## Operating Context

- **Platforms**: Modern desktop browsers (1440px+ with high-performance WebGL context) and responsive mobile devices (390px+ stacked viewports).
- **Execution Modes**: Interactive 3D exploration (pointer parallax, scrubbed scroll, interactive constellation graph) and accessible non-3D fallback modes.
- **Interactions**: Floating navigational pill, Cmd+K Command Center, interactive terminal dialogue with the Digital Twin, and direct research archives.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Single WebGL Context**: One `<Canvas>` mounted full-screen with Drei `<View.Port />` and localized `<Stage>` views tracking DOM rectangles across sections.
- **Strict Anti-Fabrication Rule**: Zero fabricated metrics, credentials, papers, or career achievements. Placeholder content must remain behind `NEXT_PUBLIC_SHOW_PLACEHOLDERS=true`.
- **Palette Discipline**: 75% dark surfaces (Obsidian `#08090C`, Carbon `#0E1117`, Graphite `#171B22`, Slate `#252C35`), 15% metallic neutrals (Titanium `#D9DEE5`, Muted Silver `#8C96A3`), 7% Ion Green accents (`#B6E36A`), and 3% Warm Bronze (`#B58A68`).
- **Typography Contract**: Space Grotesk (display/headings), Inter (body/prose), JetBrains Mono (technical metadata and status chips).
- **Quality Standards**: 100% clean passes on TypeScript typechecking, test runner (41/41 tests passing), ESLint, and Next.js Turbopack production builds.

## Product Principles

1. **Verifiable Truth First**: Never invent metrics, employers, or papers. Treat real engineering constraints with transparency and intellectual honesty.
2. **Atmospheric Substance Over Decoration**: 3D elements must be primary physical installations with tangible depth and controlled lighting, never decorative wallpapers.
3. **Restrained Accent Precision**: Ion Green is an intentional interaction and telemetry signal (≤7% of visual surface), not a color to spread everywhere.
4. **Resilient Dual-Tier Experience**: Deliver a high-framerate 3D universe for capable hardware while maintaining a fast, clean layout for reduced-motion or low-tier devices.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Respects `prefers-reduced-motion` across all Three.js render loops and Framer Motion transitions.
- High contrast ratios on all typography against obsidian and carbon surfaces.
- Accessible keyboard navigation with SkipLink and accessible ARIA landmarks.
