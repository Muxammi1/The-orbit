## Creative Direction

**Cinematic 3D Futurism × Premium Minimalism × Interactive Storytelling**
- Dark, atmospheric, sophisticated aesthetic evoking an advanced scientific research installation.
- Large-scale 3D elements integrated directly into the spatial composition.
- Editorial typography with strong compositional scale.
- Restrained color application: 75% dark surfaces, 15% metallic neutrals, 7% deliberate Ion Green accents, 3% Warm Bronze accents.

### Color System
- **Obsidian:** `#08090C` (Primary background, deep space)
- **Carbon:** `#0E1117` (Elevated surface, deep framing)
- **Graphite:** `#171B22` (Panels, modules, structure)
- **Slate:** `#252C35` (Borders, structural gridlines)
- **Ion Green:** `#B6E36A` (Information & interaction accent, energy cores)
- **Titanium:** `#D9DEE5` (Primary headings, polished metal structures)
- **Muted Silver:** `#8C96A3` (Body typography, technical labels)
- **Warm Bronze:** `#B58A68` (Warm highlight metal, accent rims)

### Typography
- **Display & Headings:** Space Grotesk
- **Body & Editorial:** Inter
- **Technical & Metadata:** JetBrains Mono

## Architecture & Technology Stack
- **Framework:** Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS v4, custom CSS token system
- **3D Graphics:** Three.js, React Three Fiber (R3F), Drei, maath
- **Animation & Transitions:** Motion (React), GSAP + ScrollTrigger
- **Backend:** FastAPI (Python), uvicorn, Pydantic, httpx
