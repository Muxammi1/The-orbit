## Problem
Large-scale examination venues require high human proctor overhead while introducing subjective variance and fatigue in monitoring protocol enforcement.

## Objectives
- Implement real-time multi-stream video ingestion across localized venue cameras.
- Detect spatial movement anomalies and unauthorized device usage with low false-positive rates.
- Maintain total on-premise processing to guarantee student privacy (zero cloud streaming).

## Architecture
Local IP camera streams are ingested via RTSP into a high-throughput video pipeline that performs frame-level spatial inference using optimized YOLO models on edge accelerators.

## Tech stack
- **Perception:** OpenCV, YOLOv8, PyTorch
- **Stream Ingestion:** GStreamer / RTSP
- **Inference Runtime:** TensorRT / ONNX Runtime
- **Platform:** Ubuntu LTS, Docker, NVIDIA Jetson / Edge GPU

## Engineering decisions
- **Local On-Premise GPU Inference over Cloud Streaming:** Absolute privacy requirement — no raw video frames are ever permitted to leave the physical institution network.
- *Rejected Alternative:* Cloud-based computer vision APIs were evaluated and formally rejected due to student privacy compliance, bandwidth constraints, and egress costs.
- **Bounding-Box Stream Logging over Full Video Recording:** Only spatial metadata and event timestamps are archived; raw video is flushed after sliding temporal verification windows.

## Limitations
- Extreme lighting variances or sharp direct sunlight can occasionally affect detection confidence.
- Occlusion in multi-row tiered auditoriums requires multi-angle camera triangulation.
