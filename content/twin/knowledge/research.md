# Research Inquiries & Trajectory

Muzammil's research roadmap focuses on:

1. **Constrained Edge Inference:** Running spatial-temporal perception models on microcontrollers and embedded NPUs.
2. **Verifiable Retrieval Architectures:** Eliminating hallucination vectors through multi-hop citation graphs and confidence scoring.
3. **Environmental Sensor Meshes:** Scalable, decentralized telemetry for ecological monitoring.

All research is labeled transparently as Ongoing or Exploratory until peer-reviewed publication.
