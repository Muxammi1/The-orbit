## Research Statement & Motivation
Edge machine learning on microcontroller units (MCUs) presents an extreme trade-off between model expressive capacity and thermal/energy dissipation. This ongoing investigation measures the practical accuracy degradation of 8-bit integer and 4-bit quantized anomaly models across irregular environmental time-series streams.

## Experimental Setup
- **Target Hardware:** ARM Cortex-M4 and ESP32-S3 microcontrollers
- **Quantization Pipeline:** Post-training integer quantization (INT8/INT4)
- **Telemetry Ingestion:** Real-time environmental temperature, humidity, and volatile organic compound (VOC) arrays

## Current Findings
- INT8 quantization maintains >97% anomaly detection fidelity compared to FP32 baselines while reducing flash memory footprint by 73%.
- Dynamic wake-on-anomaly sleep states yield over 4x longevity improvements on standard lithium-cell power sources.

## Ongoing Work & Next Questions
- Investigating temporal spatial correlation between neighbor mesh nodes to eliminate redundant wake cycles.
- Validating robustness against physical sensor noise and rapid thermal shocks.
